# THE FOUR — FIND YOUR FOUR

A WAWO Brand House campaign-platform prototype for **The Four: No One Fights Alone**.

## Campaign
**FIND → CREATE → SHARE → WATCH**

The experience is designed to turn friendship into personalised campaign content, consented registration, social sharing and a path toward cinema attendance.

## Current build
The repository now contains the first mobile-first interactive prototype.

### Planned next
- image upload / camera capture
- personalised THE FOUR campaign-art generation
- unique Four IDs and invite links
- member registration and consent flow
- cinema/date/showtime selection and ticketing handoff
- reward qualification and QR redemption
- campaign dashboard and analytics

## Rights & approvals
Official movie artwork, talent likenesses, ticketing integrations, privacy language and final reward mechanics require approval from the relevant rights holders and campaign partners.
